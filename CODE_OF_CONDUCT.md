# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone contributing to VYRO — employees, contractors, freelancers and outside
contributors — is entitled to a harassment-free experience regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume competence and good intent; ask before assuming a mistake.
- Critique code and decisions, never people.
- Prefer written, reviewable decisions over informal side channels.
- Respect athlete data. Health data is sensitive; treat it accordingly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing other people's
private information, and sustained disruption of reviews or discussions.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports are handled
confidentially and reviewed within five business days.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban any contributor whose
behaviour violates this document. Enforcement decisions are final.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
